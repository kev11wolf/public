Family field notes

Bring a six-foot leash, collapsible water bowl, waste bags, water shoes, towels, shade, sunscreen, bug spray, and a carrier for natural-surface trails. Boat ramps are vehicle zones: toddler hand-in-hand and Clyde on a short leash. Lake swimming is unguarded and shorelines can drop abruptly, so stay within arm’s reach.
