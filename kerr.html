Before leaving camp: Drive times are planning estimates from Henderson Point—not navigation. Recheck seasonal hours, beach conditions, pet rules, trail access, boat rentals, restaurant patios, and fuel availability. Dogs should be attended on a six-foot leash and are not permitted on designated swimming beaches.

Family field notes

Bring a six-foot leash, collapsible water bowl, waste bags, water shoes, towels, shade, sunscreen, bug spray, and a carrier for natural-surface trails. Boat ramps are vehicle zones: toddler hand-in-hand and Clyde on a short leash. Lake swimming is unguarded and shorelines can drop abruptly, so stay within arm’s reach.
