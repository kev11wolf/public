What Bend changes

Primary deltas
  • Oregon income tax is materially higher at this household income; Oregon’s lack of general sales tax offsets only part of that difference.
  • Realistic dog-friendly family rent creates a second-housing problem unless the Raleigh home produces income or the employer supports Bend housing.
  • Daily outdoor access becomes radically better: river corridors, waterfalls, trails, skiing, volcanoes, lakes, and camping are regular-life options.
  • Smaller-city tradeoffs matter for a stay-at-home parent: fewer indoor options and specialty services, more weather/smoke planning, and a need to intentionally build community.
  • Most spectacular Washington, Vancouver, and California destinations are road-trip scale—not casual weekends.

Strongest practical path

Target a 30–60 day furnished, dog-friendly trial rental first. If the Raleigh house can be professionally managed and approximately break even, a $230k–$240k base is the clean permanent-role target. If base is lower, require housing support in writing.

What to keep private

Your future Raleigh leasing strategy is personal financial planning. The negotiation should be based on relocation scope, Bend family housing cost, tax treatment, job travel expectations, and the value you bring—not on whether you choose to collect rent from your existing home.

What to ask payroll / HR in writing

  • Is the role expected to last less than one year, one year, or longer?
  • What is the official work location and official tax-home treatment?
  • Is lodging direct-billed, reimbursed at actual cost, paid at per diem, or given as taxable wages?
  • Are housing and relocation benefits grossed up for income/payroll tax?
  • Are spouse, child, pet, utilities, parking, furniture, deposits, and lease fees covered or excluded?
  • What happens if the project extends past the expected duration?
  • Can the company pay for Raleigh return travel and a scouting trip?
  • What receipts, deadlines, and excess-return rules apply under the expense plan?

Potential-employer offer framework

Public job materials indicate a comprehensive benefits package is plausible—medical, dental, vision and a 401(k) are common listed components—but do not assume bonus, housing, relocation, per diem, phone allowance, PTO, or travel benefits without an offer-specific written confirmation.

Preferred offer: 1–2 year role

Best fit for your stated uncertainty

Base: $230k–$240k, depending on the verified net economics of the Raleigh rental.

Housing bridge: employer-paid furnished, dog-friendly 2–3BR Bend rental for 6–12 months, including utilities, internet, parking, pet fees/deposit, furniture and tenant insurance support. If paid as taxable cash, require a gross-up.

Risk protection: scouting trip, movers/storage, lease-break protection, family return travel every 6–8 weeks, and written return-relocation help if project need changes.

Why: A 1–2 year term is long enough that you should not rely on temporary per diem as the core financial plan.

Acceptable lower-base offer

Only with defined support

Base: $205k–$225k can be economically acceptable only if the employer absorbs a large amount of your Bend housing cost in a way that is clear and durable.

Minimum structure: full furnished housing or a tax-grossed-up cash benefit that nets approximately $3,000–$3,500/month, plus moving/storage and a defined end/return plan.

Do not accept vague wording: “relocation may be available,” “reasonable expenses,” or “we can discuss per diem later” does not solve a two-household family budget.

Rental areas and site commute logic

The site reference area is northeast Bend near the Cooley Road / NE 18th Street corridor. Actual door-to-door time must be driven at your work hours; these are planning characterizations, not commute promises.

Northeast Bend / Mountain View / Boyd Acres

Best site access

Fit: best practical choice for mostly onsite hybrid work near the site. Efficient for north Bend, Redmond Airport, Smith Rock, Highway 97, and functional family rental inventory.

Tradeoff: less immediate access to the west-side trail identity and Mt. Bachelor/Cascade Lakes direction. You will still use them—but as planned outings.

Use if: commute reliability, garage/yard potential, airport trips, and rental value matter most.

Tradeoff: likely a longer site commute than NE Bend and potentially higher rent/smaller yards.

Use if: the family’s quality of day-to-day life is worth a moderate commute premium. This is the best first short-term base for testing Bend.

Tradeoff: typically higher rental premium and a longer cross-town site commute. Mountain-corridor road/weather conditions add friction in winter.

Honest geography

Bend is an exceptional Central Oregon base. It is not a shortcut to everywhere in the West. Use Bend for frequent local mountain/river/camping life; use Washington, Vancouver, Yosemite, Olympic, and the Canadian coast as deliberate 3–7 day family trips.

First six weekends by rental area

Built around short wins, toddler sleep, dog logistics, and finding your normal rhythm—not checking off attractions.

NE Bend rental

  1. Site commute test + downtown/Drake Park orientation.
  2. Smith Rock early morning; Crooked River lower trail; lunch in Redmond.
  3. Tumalo State Park and Deschutes River campground scouting.
  4. Sisters lunch and one short Cascade viewpoint/walk.
  5. High Desert Museum + Lava Lands light half-day.
  6. Old Mill and Southwest Bend drive test: decide how often you would make the cross-town recreation trip.

Weekly rhythm: let north/east convenience win on weekdays; deliberately protect one west/south outdoor outing every 1–2 weekends.

Old Mill / Southern Crossing rental

  1. Riverbend/Farewell Bend/Old Mill daily-life loop.
  2. Downtown, Drake Park, library, and toddler routine test.
  3. Tumalo Falls morning.
  4. Sunriver family day; assess whether south-of-Bend life appeals.
  5. Newberry/Lava Lands half-day.
  6. Smith Rock morning, then Redmond lunch/errands.

Weekly rhythm: river/stroller walks and library options during the week; alternate a west-side weekend with a southbound lake/campground weekend.

Southwest Bend rental

  1. Old Mill/downtown orientation and grocery route.
  2. Tumalo Falls viewpoint and short walk.
  3. Shevlin Park and Phil’s Trail reconnaissance.
  4. Mt. Bachelor / Cascade Lakes seasonal scouting drive.
  5. Sisters day trip with one short outdoor stop.
  6. Smith Rock early, then easy return day.

Weekly rhythm: this is the easiest base for skiing, forests, and lakes. Avoid the temptation to overfill every weekend just because the access is excellent.

Southeast Bend rental

  1. SE parks, grocery/medical drive, Old Mill orientation.
  2. Lava Lands and volcanic landscape half-day.
  3. Sunriver and LaPine State Park scouting.
  4. Paulina Lake or East Lake route exploration when open.
  5. Crater Lake one-night trial trip.
  6. Smith Rock or Sisters for a northbound contrast weekend.

Weekly rhythm: use the rental savings and southbound efficiency for Newberry, LaPine, Sunriver, lakes, and camping; schedule west-side trail days intentionally.
